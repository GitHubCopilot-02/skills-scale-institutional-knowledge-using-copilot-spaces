# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory contains OctoAcme’s core project management process documentation. It is designed to provide a single entry point for contributors, project leaders, and stakeholders to understand how projects are initiated, planned, executed, released, and continuously improved. Together, these documents centralize institutional knowledge and make project operations easier to discover, reuse, and evolve.

OctoAcme uses a lifecycle-based workflow that starts with initiation and planning, then moves through execution, release, and retrospective improvement. In initiation, teams validate business need, define measurable outcomes, identify stakeholders, and confirm readiness to proceed. During planning, approved work is decomposed into prioritized backlog items with acceptance criteria, estimates, dependencies, and milestone-based release planning.

Execution emphasizes consistent team rhythm and transparent tracking. Teams use project boards to manage workflow states, follow pull request and CI expectations, and coordinate through recurring standups, syncs, demos, and stakeholder updates. Roles are clearly defined across Project Managers, Product Managers, Developers, QA, and Stakeholders so ownership, decision-making, and accountability remain explicit across the delivery lifecycle.

Quality assurance and risk management are embedded throughout delivery rather than treated as a final step. Automated tests, linting, security scans, integration checks, and smoke tests support release readiness, while risk registers, escalation paths, and communication templates keep teams aligned on blockers and decisions. After each sprint, release, or incident, retrospectives capture learnings and action items so process improvements can be tracked and incorporated into future work.
